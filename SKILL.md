# Academic Writing Skill

## Objective
Help researchers improve their academic writing
using appropriate scholarly expressions.

## Instructions
1. Understand the research topic.
2. Identify the writing task.
3. Read references/academic-phrases.md.
4. Select relevant academic expressions.
5. Integrate phrases naturally.
6. Preserve the author's original meaning.
7. Avoid unnecessary repetition.
8. Never invent references or evidence.

## Supported Writing Tasks
- Research papers
- Literature reviews
- Research proposals
- Critical analysis
- Academic essays
- Conclusions

## Writing Principles
- Clarity
- Coherence
- Academic precision
- Analytical depth
- Originality
