# Academic Writing Skill — Test Prompts

## Test 1: Academic Vocabulary
Rewrite the following paragraph in a scholarly style suitable for International Relations research. Preserve the original meaning and do not introduce new evidence.

"Countries compete for resources because resources are important for their economies. This competition affects international relations."

## Test 2: Sentence Structure
Improve the academic vocabulary, sentence structure, and coherence of the following paragraph without adding unsupported claims.

"Zimbabwe has lithium resources, and foreign companies are investing in mining. The government wants to benefit more from lithium, but there are problems with processing minerals locally. Local communities are also concerned about the impacts of mining."

## Test 3: Critical Analysis
Critically analyse the statement in approximately 150 words, presenting potential benefits and limitations, distinguishing claims from evidence, and avoiding invented references.

"Foreign investment in Zimbabwe's lithium mining sector promotes economic development."

## Evaluation Criteria
- Accuracy and preservation of meaning
- Precision of academic vocabulary
- Clarity and variety of sentence structure
- Logical coherence
- Appropriate caution and qualification
- No fabricated evidence or references
